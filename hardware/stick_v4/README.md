# Stick v4 (simple) — third-party grip note

`stick_v4.py` builds our own parts only (U-joint base, spring centring, gate lid, stop collar, grip adapter).
Page: `docs/stick_v4.html`.

## F-16 grip (NOT in this repository)
- "F-16 Sidestick Grip" by **Spock** — https://www.printables.com/model/233472 (originally https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4544115).
  Hat switches: https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:4543158 (the same hat files are in the Printables zip `hat_switch_files_incl_step_and_sldprt.zip`).
- Licence: **CC BY-NC-SA** (read on the Printables page 2026-11-06). Non-commercial, attribution, share-alike.
- Because this repository is public and the licence is non-commercial, the grip STL/STEP files are **not** copied here.
  Download them from the source page and print them yourself.
- Our `grip_adapter` was sized from the author's `Viper Grip.STEP`: mount bore Ø25.0 straight up from the grip underside
  (free of internals for about 30 mm), plus a Ø32 groove 3–6 mm above the underside over the front and back 90° sectors.
  The adapter's lugs sit in that groove and are captured when the two grip halves are screwed together.
  The adapter is our own design (no geometry copied); `grip_proxy.stl` is only a placeholder cylinder for the viewer.
